Cardio Exercise

Boost your endurance, burn calories, and improve heart health.

What is Cardio?

Cardiovascular exercise (cardio) refers to any continuous activity that increases your heart rate and breathing rate while using large muscle groups. It strengthens your heart and lungs, conditioning your cardiovascular system to deliver oxygen to working muscles more efficiently.

Why is Cardio Important?

  • Heart Health: Strengthens the heart muscle and lowers blood pressure.
  • Weight Management: Burns calories to aid in fat loss and weight control.
  • Stamina & Energy: Boosts endurance and decreases fatigue over time.
  • Mental Well-being: Releases endorphins to reduce stress and improve mood.
