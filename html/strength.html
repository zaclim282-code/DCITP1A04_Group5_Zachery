Upper Body

Barbell Bench Press

4 sets · 6-10 reps

Lower the bar to your mid-chest with control, keep your shoulder blades pinned back, and drive it back up.

Upper Body

Lat Pulldown

3 sets · 10-12 reps

Pull the bar down toward your upper chest, leading with your elbows, then let it rise back under control.

Upper Body

Barbell Overhead Press

3 sets · 6-8 reps

Press the bar straight up from your shoulders, keeping your ribs down and core braced the whole way.

Lower Body

Barbell Back Squat

4 sets · 6-10 reps

Rest the bar across your upper back, sit down and back between your hips, and drive up through your heels.

Lower Body

Leg Press

3 sets · 10-15 reps

Push the platform away until your legs are nearly straight, then lower it back under control without locking your knees hard.

Lower Body

Romanian Deadlift

3 sets · 8-10 reps

Push your hips back with a soft knee bend, keep the bar close to your legs, and stop once you feel a hamstring stretch.

Core

Cable Woodchopper

3 sets · 12 reps per side

Rotate through your torso, not just your arms, pulling the cable diagonally from high to low across your body.

Core

Hanging Leg Raise

3 sets · 10-12 reps

Hang from the bar and curl your legs up using your lower abs, not momentum, then lower them slowly.

Core

Decline Weighted Sit-Up

3 sets · 12-15 reps

Hold a plate to your chest and curl up smoothly from your ribs, exhaling on the way up, without yanking your neck.
