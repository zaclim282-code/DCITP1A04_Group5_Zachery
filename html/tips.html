Fitness Tips

Small habits, big results.

Daily Habits
  • Drink at least 2 litres of water every day.
  • Eat a healthy breakfast before classes.
  • Take the stairs instead of the lift whenever possible.
  • Stretch for 5–10 minutes each morning.
Study Break Moves
  • Walk around campus for 5 minutes.
  • Do 15 squats during your break.
  • Stretch your neck, shoulders, and back.
  • Roll your wrists and ankles to reduce stiffness.
Weekend Routine
  • Go for a 30-minute jog or brisk walk.
  • Try beginner workout at home.
  • Prepare healthy meals for the week.
  • Take time to relax before the new week..
🍎 Healthy Eating
  • Never skip breakfast.
  • Choose grilled food.
  • Drink more water.
  • Eat more fruits and vegetables.
😴 Sleep Tips
  • Sleep 7–9 hours.
  • Avoid screens before bed.
  • Sleep at the same time daily.
  • Rest after workouts.
🎯 Motivation
  • Set small weekly goals.
  • Exercise with a friend.
  • Track your progress.
  • Celebrate achievements.
❤️ Your habits today shape your health tomorrow.
