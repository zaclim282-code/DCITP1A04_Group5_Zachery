About SPFIT

SPFIT explores what makes a workout stick, builds routines that fit real schedules, and helps everyone find fitness that feels like theirs.

SPFIT started as a class project with one goal: make it easy for anyone, at any fitness level, to find a workout they can actually stick to. No jargon, no guesswork — just clear plans and honest guidance.

1. The Idea

A class assignment turned into a real question: why do so many people quit a workout routine in the first two weeks?

2. The Build

Four of us split the work — design, content, functionality, and testing — and put it together page by page.

3. The Launch

SPFIT went from wireframes to a working site with quizzes, a planner, and a full library of workouts.
