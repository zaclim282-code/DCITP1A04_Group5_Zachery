About SPFIT

Built by a small team who wanted fitness to feel less intimidating

Our Purpose

SPFIT started as a class project with one goal: make it easy for anyone, at any fitness level, to find a workout they can actually stick to. No jargon, no guesswork — just clear plans and honest guidance.

🎯

Our Mission

Give beginners a clear, judgment-free starting point and give returning users the tools to keep their routine consistent.

🌱

Our Vision

A community where fitness feels approachable — where the first workout is just as welcome as the hundredth.
